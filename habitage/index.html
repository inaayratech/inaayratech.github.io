Every habit is a journey

Thirty days.
Your pace. Your words.

Pick a habit and a world to take it through. Each day you keep it, the journey moves on. Thirty kept days and it’s complete, however long that takes you.

At your own pace

No countdown and no drifting back. A missed day doesn’t end anything; the journey simply waits for you.

A pledge in your own words

“I’ll walk at sunrise, because the mornings are mine.”

Write it once. It sails with you every day.

Streaks that glow

Keep going and your streak burns a little brighter. And whatever happens, the days you’ve kept stay kept.

Day1

  1. The lagoon is asleep.

    Day one. You make a quiet promise, and Aurel steps into a world still in the dark.

  2. Every kept day lights something.

    A lantern. A bell. A frost-flower. Each day you keep your habit, the world answers.

  3. Miss a day? It waits.

    No penalties, no drifting back. Thirty kept days complete the journey, at whatever pace your life allows.

  4. The worlds keep changing.

    Aurora skies, reefs, cloud-high isles. Every chapter is a new place, and every streak a little brighter.

  5. Chapter by chapter.

    “The wind is carrying Aurel east now.” The night sky slowly turns toward morning.

  6. Day thirty. The first light wakes.

    Journey complete. “It was never meant to shine alone.”

Your companion

Meet Aurel.

A small, gentle traveller made of light, who walks every journey with you. Aurel doesn’t nag and never scolds. Show up, and Aurel carries your light into the world.

Every kept day, Aurel wakes one more thing on the long road to the first dawn.

Thirty chapter worlds

One step for every kept day.

Lagoons of lotus lanterns, aurora ice, an orrery above the clouds, a stair of last stars. Light each world, and the way on opens.

Aurel’s Home

Floating isles, asleep since the dawn.

Thirty-five old wonders stand in the mist. Every habit you keep earns sunlight, and sunlight wakes them, stage by stage: windmills, a lighthouse, an observatory, a whole sky fairground.

Asleep
