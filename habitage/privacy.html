Privacy Policy

Habitage is a calm place to keep habits — alone or with friends. This policy explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how you stay in control.

Effective September 26, 2026 · Version 2.0 · Applies to Habitage 2.0 and later on iPhone and Android.

The short version

  • You need an account (Apple, Google or email) so your journeys follow you across devices and so friends and crews can work.
  • We collect what the app needs to run, plus usage and crash data to improve it — which you can turn off at any time, and which we ask for first in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland.
  • No ads. We never sell or share your data for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your public profile shows only your name, handle, avatar, bio and a few lifetime stats. Everything else about you is private to you.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. What was only yours is erased; what you shared with others stays, shown as "Deleted user".

1. Who we are

Habitage is made by Inaayra Tech LLC, Alabama, United States ("we", "us"). We are responsible for your personal data under this policy. Contact us any time at support@inaayratech.com.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in to Habitage can see your public profile. Only people who can find you — your friends, crews, or anyone searching if you leave "Discoverable" on in Settings — will come across it. Turn Discoverable off and you won't appear in search; a friend you send your invite link to can still add you.
  • Your crew sees the habit you keep together, your progress on it, and crew chat.
  • Watchers — friends you add to a private habit — see that habit's journey and progress, and can cheer you on.
  • Only you see your private account details, your other habits, your sunlight, isles, house, Sightings and Moments.
  • Our team accesses personal data only to run and support the service, investigate a safety report, or when the law requires it.

5. Usage and crash data — your choice

Usage and crash data helps us improve Habitage. It is never sold and never used for ads.

  • In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, nothing is collected until you say yes — we ask once, when you first arrive in the app, and "Not now" is as easy as "Allow".
  • Elsewhere it is on by default.
  • Everywhere, you can change your mind at any time in Settings → Data.

6. Notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled on your device. Social notifications (a friend request, a cheer, a message, a crew moment) are sent through our servers. Choose which you get in Settings → Notifications, or turn them off in your device settings.

7. Legal bases (EU, EEA, UK)

  • Contract — to provide the app you signed up for: your account, habits, social features and subscription.
  • Consent — for usage and crash data where the law requires it; you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep Habitage secure, prevent abuse and handle safety reports.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose data by law.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, we erase it as described in section 9, straight away. Deleted data may remain in routine backups or logs for up to 30 days before it's gone. Usage data is kept for no longer than 14 months, crash reports for about 90 days. Records of moderation decisions are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. If you use Sign in with Apple on iPhone, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once, and we revoke Habitage's access to your Apple ID.

What is erased

Your account and sign-in details, your private account details, your habits, journeys and check-ins, streaks, the cheers you received, sunlight, your isles and house, chapter progress, Sightings, Moments, notifications, device registrations, and any pending friend requests or crew invites to or from you.

What stays, shown as "Deleted user"

Things you shared with other people stay part of their history: accepted friendships, messages you sent, cheers you sent, and your past activity in crews and chats. Your name and handle are removed from your profile and nobody can find, message or add you afterwards. If you want something specific removed, email support@inaayratech.com.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — Apple or Google bill it. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download (portability), delete or restrict your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You can do much of this yourself in the app (Settings, Personalization, Delete account). For anything else — including a copy of your data — email support@inaayratech.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're in the EU, EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we don't sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information, and we won't discriminate against you for using these rights.

11. Children

Habitage is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you're under the age required to consent to data processing where you live, use Habitage only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is restricted by strict server-side rules — for example, your private account details can be read only by you. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

13. International transfers

Habitage is run from the United States and our providers may process data there and in other countries. Where the law requires, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
